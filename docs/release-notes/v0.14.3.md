## What's new in Socrates 0.14.3

**Fixes**
- A tall question can now be scrolled to its end with a trackpad or mouse wheel without it folding away at the top edge.
- The empty gap that was left under the chat after a question folded is gone.
- The question dock opens and closes more smoothly, because the chat above no longer redraws while you resize it.

Installed apps update themselves.

## What's new in Socrates 0.14.0

**Sources you can check**
- When the tutor uses something from your saved notes, the sentence now ends with a small numbered marker. Click it to see the note, the page or line, and the actual passage, with a button to open the file. The passage always comes from your notes, never from the AI, so you can tell when a claim is really yours.

**Ask while a quiz is waiting**
- A slim bar above a question lets you ask the tutor something without answering. You get a reply right away, the question stays open, and your side question is kept with the record.

**Choose your models**
- Settings → Providers has a checklist for each provider, so the model menus only list the ones you use. Long model names now wrap instead of being cut off.

**Fixes**
- Gemini no longer fails on follow-up tool calls in longer sessions.

Installed apps update themselves.

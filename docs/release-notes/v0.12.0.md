## What's new in Socrates 0.12.0

**Scanned and handwritten PDFs just work**
- Importing a PDF with scanned or handwritten pages no longer asks you to do OCR yourself. With a Gemini key set, those pages are read from their picture and added to your map. Without a key, Socrates tells you to add one in Settings → Keys.
- If reading stops halfway, the file stays saved and you're told why. Nothing half-read reaches your map.
- A new **reading queue** at the top of the Library shows every file being read or added to the map, imports and watched-folder files alike, with steps like "Reading scanned page 5 of 12", waiting, and failed rows. It only appears while there is something to show.

**A calmer question card**
- Answers are lettered tiles, with a check on the one you picked and the others fading back.
- "I don't know" is a small, quiet button.
- Confidence is a clean strip, "How sure are you?", with Guess, Coin flip and Certain under the slider.
- The "Your thinking" box looks like the message box, with Dictate, usage, model and effort tucked inside it next to the button, which says what it will do: "Pick an answer", "Submit" or "Show me the answer".

Installed apps update themselves.

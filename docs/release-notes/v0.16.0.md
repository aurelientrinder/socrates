## What's new in Socrates 0.16.0

**A calmer Today page**
- Your next study step is now one big card with Start and "Only 5 minutes" right under it. Later steps sit under "Then", and exams appear once, in "Coming up", with a colour showing whether your plan covers them.
- Steps tick off as you do them, class dots show which class each one belongs to, and you can click any day of the week to see its sessions.
- A short checklist guides you through your first run.

**Start the next step with one click**
- The Start button in the sidebar now begins the next step of your plan. The small arrow next to it opens the full start dialog.
- The start dialog now asks you to be honest about how well you know the topic before you begin.

**Everything for a class in one place**
- Each class has its own page with its notes, sessions, exams and plan.
- The sidebar can group your sessions by date or by class, and each session's icon takes its class colour.

**Settings, rebuilt**
- Settings are grouped into Studying, App and Setup, with a search box, plain-language rows and theme previews. Rarely used options are tucked into "Advanced".
- Calendar and reminders now live in Settings.

**A quieter look**
- Ink replaces blue across the app.

Installed apps update themselves.

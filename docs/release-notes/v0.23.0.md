## What's new in Socrates 0.23.0

**Unified memory, FIRe & FSRS-6**
- Lessons, knowledge-graph propositions, and flashcards now share one retention model — reviewing a flashcard updates the proposition it tests, and newly generated flashcards automatically link to your note's graph nodes.
- Graph-propagated spaced repetition (FIRe) gives conservative implicit credit to solid prerequisites when you pass a dependent concept, and flags shaky prerequisites first before you drill higher-level topics.
- Upgraded the spaced-repetition scheduler from FSRS-4.5 to FSRS-6 (21 parameters including same-day review dynamics and trainable decay), with optional personal weight fitting on the Progress page once you have 100+ reviews.
- New sessions and reviews automatically receive a compact diagnostic brief of your recent misconceptions and shaky prerequisites so the tutor can target them immediately.

**Encrypted folder sync & mobile companion**
- Sync your workspace locally through any synced folder (iCloud Drive, Syncthing, Dropbox, etc.) using AES-256-GCM encryption from Settings → Sync.
- Added an installable Mobile Companion PWA (`/mobile`) for offline-capable flashcard bursts that queue and sync back when you reconnect.

**Side-by-side sources, Study Flow & document-first lessons**
- Open your source note or PDF side-by-side with the tutor (`Source` button in the lesson top bar), search extracted text, and highlight any passage to quote it directly into the composer.
- Launch a timed **Study Flow** (15, 25, or 45 minutes) from Today to move smoothly through warm-up flashcards, due reviews, and new learning in one guided session.
- Lessons now read like living documents: covered sections collapse cleanly into compact cards, a sticky state strip tracks your current section and progress, and the right-hand plan rail acts as a clickable table of contents.

Installed apps update themselves.

## What's new in Socrates 0.15.1

**Fixes**
- The reMarkable button from 0.15.0 was missing from the question card. It now sits left of Submit in the answer box. Start Screen Share on your tablet, open the reMarkable app, press the button, pick its window once, write your reasoning, and press "Use this page" to add it to "Your thinking".

Installed apps update themselves.

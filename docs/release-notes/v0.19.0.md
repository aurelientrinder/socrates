## What's new in Socrates 0.19.0

**Side threads for questions during a quiz**
- Ask the tutor something while a quiz is waiting and the answer opens in a side thread, in a panel on the right. The quiz stays open in the main chat.
- The main chat shows a small "Side thread" link where the quiz sits, placed before the quiz and your answer, since you asked first. Click it to reopen the thread.
- The tutor reads the whole session and the open quiz when it answers, without giving the quiz answer away.

**A cleaner, more compact chat**
- Thinking is now a quiet line with an animated brain while the tutor works, instead of a box. Click it to see each step along a thin trace.
- The input box, quiz card and answered quizzes are tighter, with smaller corners and less padding.

**Smoother quizzes**
- Fixed lag and glitches in the quiz card, and made the app stay responsive while the tutor works.

Installed apps update themselves.

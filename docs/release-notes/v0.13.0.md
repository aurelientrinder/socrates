## What's new in Socrates 0.13.0

**A tidier sidebar**
- Sessions read subject first, with a small tile for what kind they are: learn, notes, practice, review, and an amber tile for weak-spot drills. A count chip shows how many items are inside.
- Sessions are grouped under Today, This week and Earlier. A dot shows when one is waiting for you, search sits behind an icon, and Settings and Background agents share one footer row.

**One font everywhere**
- The whole app, lessons and titles included, now uses a single typeface. The typeface picker and the serif session-name switch are gone.

**A calmer chat box and top bar**
- The "How sure are you?" strip sits on top of one card with the Answer button, your reading notes inside it, and the usage line quietly underneath.
- The top bar shows the session kind and subject with "Teaching · step N of M", and the Plan and Finish buttons now match. The plan panel no longer sits under the window controls.
- Selected plan steps, the progress line and the timer use warm ink tints instead of blue.

**A new app icon**
- Socrates has a new mark, on every platform.

Installed apps update themselves.

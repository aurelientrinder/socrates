## What's new in Socrates 0.15.2

**Your reMarkable page goes to the tutor as a picture**
- When you press "Use this page", your handwriting is no longer converted to text. The tutor sees the page itself, so diagrams and maths come through as you drew them. A small preview stays in your answer box and in the finished card. It works with any tutor model that can see images. With a text-only model, the page is read into text with your Gemini key instead.

Installed apps update themselves.

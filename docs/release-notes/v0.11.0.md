## What's new in Socrates 0.11.0

**Watch a notes folder**
- In the **Library**, choose a **Notes folder**, for example the one your reMarkable (or Drive/Dropbox) exports to, with one subfolder per class.
- Each class can work **one by one**, **all at once**, or **automatically** as new files appear. Nothing is read until you set a folder up.
- Handwritten pages are read with Gemini; typed pages are read for free. Every file shows its state, and finished files move into a `Processed` folder.
- No page is ever read twice: copies, renamed files and re-exports only cost for genuinely new pages, and changed pages wait for your say-so.

**Faster to open**
- The window opens straight away on a small start-up screen instead of a blank wait.
- The first screen after launch is quicker, because your chat list is remembered between launches.

**Fixes and safety**
- **Retry** on the last tutor reply, plus "Try again" when a reply fails.
- If your settings file is ever damaged, it is kept aside, the latest good copy is restored, and Today tells you.
- Review time estimates now agree between Today and Progress, and the over/underconfident verdict waits for 5 answers instead of 3.

Installed apps update themselves.

## What's new in Socrates 0.15.0

**Write your answers on your reMarkable**
- While a question is open, a new reMarkable button sits next to Submit. Start Screen Share on your tablet, pick the reMarkable app's window once, and you see a live view of your page. Press "Use this page" and your handwriting is read and added to "Your thinking", ready to edit before you submit. Only the window you pick is looked at, and nothing is saved. It needs your Gemini key, like the other handwriting features.

**Smoother long chats**
- Typing in the message box no longer re-draws the whole conversation, and messages far off screen cost nothing until you scroll to them.

Installed apps update themselves.

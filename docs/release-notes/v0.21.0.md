## What's new in Socrates 0.21.0

**Mock exams**
- Problems can now come with figures and graphs: a mechanics setup, a circuit, a labelled structure under the scenario, or a graph a part asks you to read values from. The examiner looks at each figure it draws and redraws it until it matches the problem, and the independent check removes any figure that contradicts it.
- Figures are drawn in ink, read well in light and dark, and open full size with a click. You still answer in text.

Installed apps update themselves.

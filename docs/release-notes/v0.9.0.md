## What's new in Socrates 0.9.0

**Class calendar**
- The **Library** has a new **Calendar** tab showing the next eight weeks of your calendar as a week grid.
- Every event is filed under a course, marked "not a class", or flagged as needing a class, with an "N of M filed" counter so nothing is silently missed.
- Click an event, or drag it onto a course, to file it. You can file every event with that title or just that one. Each change has Undo and a one-tap "File under" suggestion.
- Your choices are remembered separately from your course groups, so re-reading the calendar never undoes them. Your daily briefing, study plan and course suggestions all use them. Deleting a course frees its events.
- **Merge course groups.** Drag one class onto another, or use "Merge into…", when two groups are really the same course. There's an undo if you change your mind.

**Calendar review**
- A review shows what Socrates did with your events: the ones that **need a class**, the ones it decided are **not a class** (with the reason for each), and the ones it **filed automatically**. Correct any of them in a tap.

**Class lens**
- On the **map**, pick one class to see just its topics and what they connect to.
- The same lens now runs through the app: the **Start** dialog, **Progress by class**, colours on **Today**, and a class filter in the **Library**.

Installed apps update themselves.

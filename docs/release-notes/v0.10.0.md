## What's new in Socrates 0.10.0

**A calmer chat with your tutor**
- All the thinking behind one reply now folds into a single "Thinking · N steps" block at the top, so the reply reads as one answer.
- The conversation sits in a comfortable reading column, and your own messages show as simple rounded bubbles.
- Finished replies have a quiet **Copy** button when you hover over them.
- The message box and its tools (dictate, usage, model, effort) now share one card.

**A redesigned class calendar**
- The calendar is one card with a **Week / Month** switch.
- Your classes are chips above the grid: use them as a legend, a filter, a place to drop events to file them, or drag one onto another to merge. "Not a class" is a chip too, to show or hide those events.
- Your **study plan** shows as a row above the hours (it sets the day, not the hour), and you can switch it off.
- Exams stand out as solid blocks, and events that still need a class are clearly marked. Overlapping events no longer squash each other.
- A side panel has a small month and a "To file" list with a progress bar, suggestions and a one-tap "File suggested".

Installed apps update themselves.

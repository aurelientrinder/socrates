## What's new in Socrates 0.8.0

**Smarter studying**
- **"Why?" on teach predictions.** When the tutor asks you to predict an answer, you can now explain your reasoning, and it responds to the reasoning, not just the answer.
- **Course groups.** Organise your sessions by course so everything for one exam lives together.
- A class's lecture, tutorial and practical sessions (CM, TD, TP) now land in one course group instead of three.
- **Restore.** Bring your study history back from an automatic snapshot or an export file.

**Calendar and reminders**
- Study blocks and reminders now go to their own **Socrates calendar**, so your real calendar is never edited. Socrates only reads the rest.
- **Phone and laptop reminders** appear as alerts on that calendar.
- **One-time step:** open **Today** and press **Reconnect** to grant the new calendar permissions.

**Safer and more reliable**
- Saves are crash-safe, with a daily backup and an export option.
- Flashcards and mock exams now count toward your streak and study record.
- A deleted session keeps its study history.
- Code-based exam questions are graded without a model.
- The tutor is locked down: it can't read private files or reach private network addresses.
- Faster loading, plus a handful of Windows file-saving fixes.
- Interactive demos no longer leave a big empty space beneath them.

**Simpler setup**
- Removed the ChatGPT, Grok and Copilot subscription logins, Deepgram dictation and Brave/Tavily search to keep things focused.
- Demos work without installing anything extra.

**Windows note:** the installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn you. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Installed apps update themselves.

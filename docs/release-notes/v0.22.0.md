## What's new in Socrates 0.22.0

**Exam cheat sheets**
- Make a cheat sheet for an exam from the Start dialog (Cheat sheet), a class page or a course card: pick the class or up to five note files, and optionally say what it should focus on.
- Every line is marked with what your own tested answers show: **you know this**, **shaky** (missed, unsure, overconfident or fading, with a "Watch:" note on the trap) or **not seen yet**. The marks update as you study.
- Filter to just the shaky or the new lines, drill the shaky ones or learn the new ones in one click, and print the sheet on its own.
- A second pass checks every line against your notes before you see it. Cheat sheets use your Mock exams model.

Installed apps update themselves.

## What's new in Socrates 0.8.1

- **Course groups, fixed.** A class's lecture, tutorial and practical sessions (CM, TD, TP) now land in one course group instead of three. Each session keeps its meeting type.

Installed apps update themselves. If you haven't yet, open **Today** and press **Reconnect** once to grant the Socrates calendar permissions from 0.8.0.

## What's new in Socrates 0.20.0

**Mock exams**
- Fixed mock exams failing with "The examiner did not produce a valid exam." Socrates now nudges the model to hand in its exam, repairs small bookkeeping slips itself instead of rejecting the whole paper, and logs every step so any future failure can be diagnosed.
- New setting: Settings, Models & keys, **Mock exams**. Pick the model that writes and marks your mock exams. Until you pick one, it uses your Teaching model.

Installed apps update themselves.

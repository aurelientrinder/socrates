## What's new in Socrates 0.14.5

**Fixes**
- Long quiz and practice cards now cap at 75% of the window's height and scroll internally from the top, so a long list of answer options never pushes the question out of view.
- Quiz and practice cards now have an `X` button in the top-right corner to hide them into the bottom bar (click the bar to reopen), and no longer collapse on their own while you scroll.
- Scrolling inside an open quiz or practice card is smoother and no longer lags.

Installed apps update themselves.

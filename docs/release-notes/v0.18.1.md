## What's new in Socrates 0.18.1

**More adaptive probing and practice from your knowledge map**
- When reading your knowledge map before probing or setting practice problems, the tutor now sees how well each topic has held up (solid, due, shaky, or overconfident) along with direct prerequisite and dependent chains between concepts.
- This helps the tutor skip topics you already know well, revisit fading or overconfident areas, and check shaky prerequisites before building further.

Installed apps update themselves.
